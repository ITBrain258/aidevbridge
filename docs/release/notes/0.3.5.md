aidevbridge 0.3.5 gives the sidebar a new look: buttons on every host, a calmer status display, an icon rail when you collapse it and four more backgrounds.

- **Buttons on every host:** each host shows its buttons (Connect or Disconnect, New session, Reload sessions and the menu), expanded or collapsed. Before, only the host you had clicked last showed them. "Disconnect" is green while the connection is up, so a glowing button means connected.
- **Status display:** only what needs you stands out: Waiting, Ended and Failed. Everything else is quiet, and the color of its symbol tells the rest: green means connected, blue means the agent is working, gray means idle or offline. The words and shapes are the same as before.
- **Waiting sessions:** they sit in a card of their own at the top of the sidebar, "Waiting for you", with their number and the shortcut that jumps to the next one.
- **Collapsible sidebar:** a button at the bottom of the sidebar collapses it to a narrow icon rail that still shows your hosts, their state and the number of waiting sessions. A click on a host brings the sidebar back. Settings → Appearance → "Icon rail when collapsed" turns the rail off; Ctrl+Shift+B then hides the sidebar completely, as before. On a phone the sidebar stays the drawer it was.
- **Appearance:** four more backgrounds, Midnight, Mocha, Plum and Pine, next to Graphite and Slate. With the seven accent colors that makes 42 combinations. The bottom of the sidebar shows the app's mark and its version; the commit id is under Settings → About.
